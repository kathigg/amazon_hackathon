# Branding Directions

The app now uses an abstract in-product mark in `components/CivicConnectMark.tsx` instead of the old squirrel artwork. That keeps the visual language focused on policy coverage rather than a mascot.

## Recommended directions

1. Signal desk
   Use stacked bars, filing marks, or wire-service motifs. This matches the current "latest legislation, decoded." editorial framing.

2. Capitol grid
   Use architectural lines, committee-table geometry, or document margins. This feels institutional without leaning on flags or politicians.

3. Civic pulse
   Use a small live indicator, map pin, or district grid with motion. This reinforces the communal "our readers" positioning and the real-time congress coverage.

## Image guidance

- Prefer places, documents, infrastructure, maps, ballots, and public institutions over portraits.
- Keep bill art broad and issue-based, not candidate-based.
- Use people only when the bill is directly about a public officeholder or a named historical event.
